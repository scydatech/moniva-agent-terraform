# Moniva Operations Authorization Matrix

Document ID: MNV-GOV-001 | Version: 4.0 | Owner: Chief Operating Officer | Effective: 1 August 2026
Classification: Internal | SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR SYSTEM TESTING - FICTIONAL CONTENT

## 1. Principle
Investigators and AI tools gather evidence and recommend actions. Every consequential financial or account action requires authorization by the approver below, recorded in the case file before the action is taken.

## 2. Matrix
| Action | Up to NGN 100,000 | NGN 100,001 - 500,000 | Above NGN 500,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manual reversal of a failed transfer | Operations Supervisor | Operations Supervisor | Head of Operations |
| Refund of a duplicate debit | Operations Supervisor | Head of Operations | Head of Operations |
| Goodwill credit or fee waiver | Operations Supervisor | Head of Operations | Chief Operating Officer |
| Temporary limit increase | Operations Supervisor | Head of Operations | Not permitted |

| Action | Approver |
|---|---|
| Post-No-Debit or account freeze | Fraud Analyst, confirmed by Fraud Team Lead |
| Lifting PND or freeze | Fraud Team Lead |
| Beneficiary bank recall | Fraud Team Lead |
| Escalation to beneficiary bank for settlement issues | Settlements team |

## 3. Segregation of duties
The person who investigates a case cannot authorize the resulting action. Authorizations must be recorded with the approver's name, time and the case reference.
